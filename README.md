## 🌱 About S3 Lab

**S3 Lab (Simple Sustainable Systems Lab)** at IIT Bhilai is an interdisciplinary research group focused on building practical, scalable, and socially impactful solutions.

The lab works at the intersection of **technology, sustainability, and real-world problem solving**, aiming to design systems that are not only technically sound but also accessible and meaningful for society.

###  Core Focus Areas

*  **Natural Farming**
  Sustainable agriculture practices including soil health, water management, and ecological farming.

*  **Health Care**
  Development of affordable medical devices, AI-based diagnostics, and intelligent health systems.

*  **Industrial Safety**
  Real-time monitoring, anomaly detection, and AI-driven safety systems for industrial environments.

*  **Education**
  Activity-based learning, teacher training, and digital tools aligned with modern education policies.

###  Vision

To create **simple, sustainable, and scalable systems** that bridge the gap between research and real-world impact.

###  What We Do

* Build prototypes and deploy field solutions
* Collaborate with industry, schools, and communities
* Apply machine learning, data systems, and engineering
* Focus on usability, affordability, and sustainability
